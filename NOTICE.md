# Source and dependency attribution

The initial App is a copy of `odie/apps/insider-cat`, developed for Agent Canvas
on 14 September 2026. Its localized copy includes a subset of Agent Canvas's
translation catalog. The original Odie copy is retained.

Agent Canvas is part of [OpenHands](https://github.com/OpenHands/OpenHands).
The OpenHands MIT license and copyright notice are retained in `LICENSE`.

The prepared browser bundle includes React, React DOM, Scheduler, i18next,
react-i18next, and their bundled helpers. Their license notices are retained
inline by the build. Exact dependency versions are recorded in
`package-lock.json`; installed packages contain their full upstream licenses.
